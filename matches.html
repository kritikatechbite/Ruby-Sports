FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Are these real fixtures?

No. Every event on this page is a clearly marked demonstration. We use fictional teams, venues and dates to show how event information is presented.

Why are there no scores or live updates?

This is an information site. It does not provide live scores, odds, wagering or streaming. For results, use the official competition website.

How do I check a start time?

Look at the time zone label next to each time. New Zealand uses NZST and NZDT, and the clocks change twice a year. Organisers can also change start times, so confirm before you go.

Can I buy tickets here?

No. Ruby Sport does not sell or book tickets. Tickets are handled by the event organiser or venue.

What does the event status mean?

'Example only' means the entry is a demonstration. When verified information is published, labels such as 'Scheduled', 'Postponed' or 'Completed' will show the state of the event.
